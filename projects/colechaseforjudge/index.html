Cole Chase

Candidate for Circuit Court Judge
Klamath County, Oregon

This is a concept website draft intended to demonstrate a potential online presence for a judicial campaign.

About

Cole Chase has experience serving as a Deputy District Attorney in Klamath County, working within the criminal justice system and representing the interests of the public. His professional background includes handling cases involving public safety and legal accountability.

Approach

A judicial role requires fairness, integrity, and a commitment to applying the law consistently. This concept emphasizes a professional and balanced presentation focused on experience, responsibility, and service to the community.

Community

Strong communities rely on trust in their legal systems. A campaign website provides a space to share background, values, and information with the public in a clear and accessible way.
