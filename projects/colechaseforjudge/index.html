About

A prosecutor’s record built in service to this county.

As a prosecutor in the Klamath County District Attorney’s Office, Cole Chase has dedicated his career to serving this county. His campaign is centered on public safety, victims’ rights, accountability, and the obligation to uphold Constitutional protections.

His campaign message emphasizes courtroom experience, including trial work holding accountable those who steal, those who hurt children, and those who harm the community. The site can pair that argument with the verified filing background already listed below.

Qualifications

Built through casework, preparation, and years in the system.

Practice and public service

The filing record identifies 21 years in legal practice, current work as a Deputy District Attorney, and additional legal practice through LMT Defenders.

Teaching and judicial service

His listed background includes adjunct faculty work in criminal justice and six years as a youth peer court judge, both of which support a qualifications argument rooted in legal process and judgment.

Education

His filing lists degrees from the University of Iowa, including a BA in political science and history, an MS in urban and regional planning, and a JD from the College of Law.

Why Me

Why Cole Chase is the right choice.

Protect Constitutional rights

His campaign is centered on the claim that every Constitutional right matters and that a judge must guard those rights for every citizen who enters the courtroom.

Hold people accountable

He argues that fairness and accountability are not in conflict: everyone deserves dignity and impartial treatment, but criminals must be held responsible for the harms they cause.

Let Klamath County decide

His message draws a clear contrast between a locally chosen judge and one appointed from Salem, arguing that Klamath County voters should choose who serves on the bench.

As your Circuit Court Judge, I will:

  • Make decisions that uphold justice and protect our community’s priorities.
  • Continue to safeguard our Constitutional rights, all of them.
  • Hold individuals accountable while treating everyone with dignity and respect.
  • Uphold the rule of law with integrity and accountability.
  • Ensure every person receives fair and impartial treatment.

FAQ

Short answers to the questions judicial voters actually have.

Can judicial candidates talk about specific issues or future cases?

Not like candidates for legislative office can. The safer lane is to explain background, temperament, qualifications, and commitment to fairness rather than pre-judging disputes.

Why is Cole qualified?

He is making the case on experience and trial work: two decades practicing law, service as a Deputy District Attorney, prior youth peer court judging, criminal justice teaching, and local public service on the Klamath Falls Planning Commission.

What is this campaign about?

The campaign message is about Constitutional rights, public safety, victims, accountability, and making sure Klamath County voters choose the next judge.

What is the truth about the gun incident being discussed online?

There’s been a lot of noise on social media about the charges related to the gun incident involving Cole Chase. In this video, he sits down with a member of the community to address it face to face, on camera, with no filters.

The campaign’s position is that he pulled a gun in self-defense, was never convicted of a felony, that claims circulating online are false, and that the District Attorney’s Office hired him back because of the kind of prosecutor he is.

The point of sharing the reel here is directness: he answers the questions himself, on the record, and asks voters not to let misinformation decide the election.
